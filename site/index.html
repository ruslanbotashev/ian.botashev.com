DIGITAL LOGIC / CUSTOM ISA02

A processor,
one decision
at a time.

CG-16-1 · A custom 16-bit CPU.

I designed a graphics-capable processor in the Digital logic simulator, building it from the ground up around a custom instruction set.

The project connects the abstract idea of an instruction with the logic that carries it out. I also wrote its assembler; a processor emulator is a work in progress.
